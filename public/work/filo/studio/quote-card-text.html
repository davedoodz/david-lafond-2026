Filo quote cards on phones: text layouts

Six ways to set the interview quotes on the phone cards (278 × 470, the hang tag's proportions). A is the page today. B–F set the quote at 22–27px in a heavier Inter weight (540–600, up from 460), so the text takes about as much of the card as it does on desktop. The art is centred at 92% of the card in every option; the phone dials can still move and size it per card.
