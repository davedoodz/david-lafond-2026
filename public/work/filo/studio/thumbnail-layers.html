Filo thumbnail · layers

Ways to layer the Filo card the way the Momo card is layered: the phone on the left, the tag in the middle, the portal on the right. Each device plays a few seconds cut from the case study prototypes, not the whole flow. Behind them, every product on the wall turns into a swatch of fabric in its own colour while it spins, then back: one material, three forms.

Every card is at its real size (867 × 493) and uses the real wall from FiloCard.tsx. The controls change all the cards at once. Each swatch is a photograph of a sample of that product’s material, in its colour (generated with ChatGPT image generation, in /work/filo/thumb/fabric/): textiles are cut with pinking shears, leathers and mycelium with a knife, as in the Future Fabric catalog.
