Ways to show the material record travelling from the portal onto the tag, then from the tag into the phone. Same triptych as the layers study — desk on the right, the tag on the cord in the middle, the phone on the left, in the positions picked there — but the subject here is what moves between them: a designer publishes Bacterial Cellulose in the portal, it is written onto the tag, a technical designer's phone reads the tag and opens the record.

Each loop runs portal → tag, a short hold, then tag → phone, then rests before the next record leaves. The phone's snippet restarts right as the data lands on it, so the "Filo Tag Detected" screen appears at the moment of arrival.
