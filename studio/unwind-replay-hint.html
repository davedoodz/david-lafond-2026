Hinting that scrolling up replays the intro

Each frame is the real Unwind hero (captured from the site at 1440×900) with the live voice band from braid.js. On the site, any of these would show only when you land on the hero after the intro, and stop once you have replayed it. Hover, click or scroll inside the frames to try them; the label text is editable.

Open the live hero: /work/unwind

02

Hover to replay

Hover or click the band

The band behaves like a control: when the cursor reaches it, it swells, the cursor grows the way it does on links, and a small "Replay intro" pill drops under it. Clicking plays the zoom back to the stage.

Replay intro
FeelsPlayful and discoverable; rewards curiosity.
Clear?Only once you hover. Works on desktop, not on touch.
TradeoffAdds a click target to the band; pair with 01 or 05 for phones.

Peek above

Watch

Like a pull-to-refresh hint: the whole page eases down a few dozen pixels, showing the bottom of the phone from the intro hanging above the hero, then settles back. It shows there is something up there without saying so.

Clear?Says "there's more above"; less clear that it replays.

04

Rising wisps

Watch

Every few seconds a faint copy of the band lifts off and drifts up out of the top of the screen, and a hairline chevron above it glows as it passes. Direction without words: the voice came from up there.

FeelsAmbient and on-brand; the band is the hint.
Clear?The subtlest; some people won't connect it to scrolling.
TradeoffConstant motion near the title; slow the rate or stop after three.
05

Scroll intent

Scroll up inside the frame

Nothing shows until you scroll up. Then the band starts to grow toward the centre as the page gives way, with "Keep scrolling to replay" and a thin progress line in the band's colours. Let go early and it springs back; reach the end and the intro plays.

My pick: combine 01 and 05. The whisper caption tells first-time visitors once, and the scroll-intent stretch makes the replay feel deliberate instead of accidental. 02 is a good desktop extra if you want the band to be clickable.
