One loop: Momo is there throughout; the Watch rises in and the iPhone drops in. Heartbeat lines draw in from the card edges, behind both devices, and meet in Momo. Momo closes its eyes while eight inputs orbit it, as the voice note says a long walk by the river left you calm all day. Then it opens them, the lines go and the inputs turn into eight outputs. The iPhone switches to the tracker and the Watch links that calm to the data. The desktop card (867 × 493) and the mobile card (100 : 134) play in sync; the Selected Works card on the home page runs the same engine and the values saved here.
