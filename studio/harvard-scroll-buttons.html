Top and bottom buttons, phones

The Harvard & MIT Book tab as it is now on a phone: 31 spreads stacked. Each phone below scrolls for real, and its buttons work: up glides to the front cover, down to the back cover. Every control uses the Book / Grid switch's look: 44px frosted pill, hairline border, stone icons that turn paper-white on press.

On the site the down button would land with the back cover centred, and the up button with the cover under the nav. Hidden buttons (options 3 and 6) fade in once you leave the end they point to.
